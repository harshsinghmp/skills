# 🔬 research:competitor-analysis — Competitor Messaging Grid & Positioning Teardowns

Playbook for auditing competitor value propositions, landing page claims, pricing wedges, and extracting white-space differentiation opportunities.

---

## 1. The 5-Pillar Competitor Messaging Grid

Evaluate key market competitors across five dimensions:

| Dimension | Audit Questions | What to Extract |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Headline Promise** | What is the H1 above the fold on their homepage? | Core value proposition, target ICP angle. |
| **2. Feature Claims** | What specific capabilities do they brag about? | Table-stakes features vs. genuine differentiators. |
| **3. Pricing & Packaging** | How do they gate features across tiers? | Pricing anchor points, usage meters, free-tier limits. |
| **4. Proof Points** | What logos, customer quotes, and stats are displayed? | Market maturity, enterprise vs. SMB focus. |
| **5. The Unstated Weakness** | What do customers complain about in G2/Trustpilot reviews? | Product gaps, slow support, hidden fees, complex onboarding. |

---

## 2. White-Space Differentiation Prompts

When positioning an agency client against entrenched incumbents, test these four differentiation wedges:

1. **The Speed & Simplicity Wedge**: If competitors are bloated enterprise suites (e.g. Salesforce, Adobe), position as the zero-setup, blazingly fast alternative.
2. **The Specialization Wedge**: If competitors are generic all-in-one tools, position exclusively for one vertical (e.g. *"The CRM built exclusively for HVAC contractors"*).
3. **The Anti-Complexity Wedge**: Emphasize transparent, unbundled pricing and zero hidden contracts.
4. **The Outcome Wedge**: Sell guaranteed business results rather than software seats.
