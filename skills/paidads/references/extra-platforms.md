# extra-platforms — Secondary paid platforms: Microsoft, Pinterest, X, Apple Search, retail media, native, audio, CTV/DOOH, Local Services.

The eight primary modes own the core spend. This mode extends the same doctrine (objective before budget, audience before creative, tracking before launch) to secondary platforms. Graduate a platform to a full mode when it holds sustained standalone budget.

## Intake

- Which platform and why it fits the audience (not novelty)
- Native account access + conversion tracking status
- Budget floor for the platform's auction to exit learning

## Deliverable

Platform launch note: native object map, one targeting rule, one gotcha, spend floor, and the graduation threshold — or a no-go with reason.

## Procedure

1. Confirm audience fit first — a platform without the audience is a no-go, recorded with reason.
2. Map the doctrine to native objects per the platform notes below; verify tracking before any spend.
3. Set the platform's spend floor; below it, consolidate into a primary channel instead.
4. Review quarterly: sustained standalone budget → graduate to a full mode.

## Platform notes

- **Microsoft/Bing** — Search import from Google Ads first, then differentiate (LinkedIn-profile targeting is the edge). Gotcha: volume is a fraction of Google; judge on CPA, not scale. Starter math: 15–30 coherent phrase-match keywords per ad group, $15–30/day per campaign for the first 14 days, and 30–50 conversions before automated bidding earns trust.
- **Pinterest** — Discovery mindset, long consideration windows; catalog-style evergreen creative. Gotcha: attribution lags — spend often shows up in branded search 60+ days later, so use longer attribution windows.
- **X** — Interest and keyword targeting; brand-safety controls mandatory. Gotcha: volatility — it wins event-pegged spikes, not steady-state DTC; cap test budgets and watch placement reports.
- **Apple Search** — Exact-match brand plus competitor conquest; tiny surface, high intent. Gotcha: small keyword pool; over-segmentation kills learning.
- **Amazon plus retail media** — Bottom-funnel intent; campaigns map to SKUs, not keywords. Gotcha: retail readiness (reviews, stock) gates everything, and measurement must prove incrementality beyond owned-channel attribution.
- **Native (Taboola/Outbrain)** — Arbitrage only works with real LTV math. Gotcha: whitelist placements aggressively.
- **Audio (Spotify etc.)** — Weak click attribution; measure with promo codes and brand-lift proxies. Gotcha: never judge on last-click ROAS.
- **CTV/DOOH** — Frequency discipline is the whole game. Gotcha: set frequency caps day one.
- **Local Services** — Pay-per-lead; profile completeness outranks bidding. Gotcha: lead disputes need a documented credit process.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Audience fit stated or no-go recorded.
- [ ] Tracking verified before spend.
- [ ] Spend floor named; below-floor spend consolidated.
- [ ] Graduation threshold written for any platform kept.

## Routing

Primary-channel work → that channel's mode; account-wide issues → `audit`; landing pages → `webdev` plus `refactor-ui`.
