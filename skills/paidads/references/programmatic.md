# programmatic — Programmatic: DSP display/video buying, brand safety, supply path.

## Intake

- Objective and KPI (CPM, viewability, CPA)
- Budget floor (programmatic needs volume to optimize)
- Brand-safety requirements
- Deal structure: open auction, PMP, or programmatic guaranteed

## Deliverable

Media plan: DSP strategy (platforms, deal types), audience strategy (1st/3rd-party data segments), supply/brand-safety setup (allowlists, viewability floors, exclusion categories), measurement plan (viewability, fraud reports), and the spend pace.

## Procedure

1. Pick deal structure: open auction (reach), PMPs (quality), guaranteed (fixed placements).
2. Layer audiences: 1st-party segments first, 3rd-party only where 1st-party is thin.
3. Set supply controls: ads.txt verification, allowlist publishers, block MFA (made-for-advertising) inventory.
4. Viewability floors: ≥ 70% target, fraud protection (IVT) reporting on.
5. Creative: display sizes (300x250, 728x90, 970x250, 320x50), video 15/30s, all tested.
6. Pace by flight plan; monitor against pacing curve weekly.
7. Measure beyond clicks: view-through, brand-lift studies for awareness tiers.
8. Select flight schedule — continuous vs pulsing vs flighting — with heavy-up periods tied to seasonality and demand moments.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Deal type matches quality/reach tradeoff.
- [ ] MFA and unsanctioned supply blocked by default.
- [ ] Viewability + IVT monitoring live.
- [ ] 1st-party audiences used before 3rd-party.
- [ ] Creative spec'd for every placement size.
- [ ] Flight schedule chosen (continuous/pulsing/flighting) with heavy-ups tied to seasonality.
