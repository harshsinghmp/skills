# linkedin — LinkedIn Ads: firmographic targeting, sponsored content, lead-gen forms for B2B.

## Intake

- ICP definition (title, seniority, company size, industry)
- Offer strength (whitepaper vs demo vs free trial)
- Sales cycle length and lead-scoring criteria
- Budget (LinkedIn CPC is premium; floor accordingly)

## Deliverable

Campaign plan: audience definition (job title/company/industry combos with reach estimates), campaign type (sponsored content vs lead-gen forms vs message ads), creative briefs with professional-tone copy, bid/budget floor, and lead-routing plan (forms → CRM fields).

## Procedure

1. Define the ICP precisely — title+seniority+company-size beats industry alone; check the audience meter (50k–200k is workable).
2. Pick format: sponsored content (feed), lead-gen forms (in-platform conversion), message ads (permission required).
3. Write copy in professional register: lead with the business problem, zero hype; numbers > adjectives.
4. Pair with a content offer (report, benchmark, template) — cold LinkedIn converts on value, not pitch.
5. Bid strategy: manual CPC to control floor; expect premium CPC — set budget by target CPL tolerance.
6. Wire lead-gen form → CRM: hidden fields, UTM capture, routing rules and speed-to-lead SLA.
7. Retarget site visitors and engagers as a second layer (small but hot audience).
8. Pre-screen messages on a synthetic panel for resonance/clarity/credibility/urgency/differentiation before live spend.
9. Define the anti-persona with exclusion criteria, map the buying committee (champion/economic buyer/blocker), and spec the lookalike seed (source, size, recency).

## Quality gate

- [ ] Audience is firmographic, not generic interest.
- [ ] Copy passes the professional register test (numbers, no hype).
- [ ] Offer is a content asset or demo — not a cold pitch.
- [ ] Form → CRM routing defined with speed-to-lead SLA.
- [ ] Budget floor respects premium CPC reality.
- [ ] Message pre-screened (resonance/clarity/credibility/urgency/differentiation) before spend.
- [ ] Anti-persona exclusions, buying-committee map, and lookalike seed spec recorded.
