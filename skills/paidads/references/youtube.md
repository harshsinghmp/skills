# youtube — YouTube Ads: skippable in-stream, Shorts, discovery — hook-driven video structure.

## Intake

- Video assets or production plan (hook < 5s)
- Objective (awareness vs consideration vs conversion)
- Audience and placement strategy
- CPV vs conversion bid model

## Deliverable

Campaign plan: formats (skippable, non-skippable, shorts, discovery), audience strategy (custom segments, remarketing, in-market), hook-first creative brief per video, bid model per objective, and the measurement split (view-through vs click-through).

## Procedure

1. Pick formats by objective: skippable for reach+consideration, Shorts for awareness with young demos, discovery for intent capture.
2. Brief creative with the hook < 5s (skip decision happens there); brand within first 5s.
3. Build audiences: custom intent (search terms), remarketing (viewers/site), customer match.
4. Bid: target CPV for awareness, conversions/conversion-value for direct response.
5. Separate measurement: VTR + view-through for awareness; clicked-conversions for DR.
6. Plan a remarketing layer from viewers — YouTube's hottest audience is its own engagers.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Hook lands < 5s; brand visible early.
- [ ] Format mix matches objective.
- [ ] Custom-intent or remarketing audiences present.
- [ ] Bid model matches objective (no CPV for DR).
- [ ] View-through reported separately from click-through.
