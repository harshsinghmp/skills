# snapchat — Snapchat Ads: Snap/Story ads and AR lenses for younger demographics.

## Intake

- Demographic fit (13–34 skew)
- Objective (lenses are awareness plays)
- Vertical video assets 9:16
- Swipe-up destination experience

## Deliverable

Campaign plan: ad format mix (single image/multi-image/story/AR), targeting by demo/interest/lookalike, bid strategy with goal-based bidding, and the swipe-up destination (instant experience page) spec.

## Procedure

1. Confirm demo fit — Snapchat skews young; mismatch = wasted spend.
2. Pick formats: Snap Ads (9:16 video), Story Ads (3–10 cards), AR lenses (awareness/dwell).
3. Build swipe-up destination: fast, vertical, single-action instant experience.
4. Target: age band + interest + lookalike; Geo-filters for local pushes.
5. Bid: goal-based (SWT/ purchases) once events verified; else CPC floor.
6. For AR lenses: measure shares and dwell, not clicks — lens economics are awareness.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Demo fit confirmed before spend.
- [ ] All assets 9:16, destination instant and vertical.
- [ ] Format mix matches objective (lenses ≠ direct response).
- [ ] Goal-based bidding only with verified events.
- [ ] Lens KPIs are shares/dwell — not CPA.
