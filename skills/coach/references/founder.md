# 👑 Founder Leverage & Strategic Agency Scaling Mode (`coach:founder`)

> **Council Lead**: **Sol** (Product Architect & Full-Stack Automator) & **Crew** (Operations Lead)  
> **Target Scope**: Agency founders, technical principals, managing partners, and executive operators.  
> **Core Objective**: Break the founder bottleneck, transition from expert practitioner to systems architect, eliminate the feast-or-famine whiplash, and scale high-margin agency infrastructure.

## ⚡ Execution Procedure

```
┌────────────────────────────────┐     ┌────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Executive Capacity Audit    │ ──► │ 2. Delegation & Leverage Map   │
│ Time distribution across Tiers │     │ Code vs Systems vs Growth      │
└────────────────────────────────┘     └────────────────────────────────┘
                                                       │
┌────────────────────────────────┐                     ▼
│ 4. Emit Executive Memo         │ ◄── ┌────────────────────────────────┐
│ Weekly leverage score & goals  │     │ 3. Invariant & Pipeline Guard  │
└────────────────────────────────┘     │ Enforce 15% pipeline reserve   │
                                       └────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 1: Audit Weekly Capacity Distribution
Categorize founder hours across 4 operational tiers:
- **Tier 1 (Strategic Growth & Architecture)**: High-ticket sales, core system design, key hires, partner relationships.
- **Tier 2 (Systems & Automation)**: Codifying SOPs, training team leads, building internal agent harnesses.
- **Tier 3 (Operational Management)**: Routine project status meetings, basic ticket assignment, invoice follow-ups.
- **Tier 4 (Tactical Triage & Craft)**: Writing basic application code, fixing minor CSS bugs, handling ad-hoc client pings.

### Step 2: Enforce the 70/30 Leverage Invariant
A scaling agency founder must maintain:
- $\ge 70\%$ bandwidth in **Tier 1 & Tier 2**.
- $\le 30\%$ bandwidth in **Tier 3 & Tier 4**.
If Tier 4 exceeds 30%, coach flags an active **Founder Bottleneck Alert** and generates an explicit delegation plan for designated team leads.

### Step 3: Audit Pipeline & Pre-Flight Deals
- Verify that 15% weekly pipeline capacity was maintained.
- Audit inbound leads against the **Pre-Flight Scorecard** (Budget, Authority, Timeline, Stack Alignment, Mutual Respect).

### Step 4: Emit Weekly Founder Memo
Compile executive summary logging operational leverage score, codified principles, and upcoming strategic milestones.
