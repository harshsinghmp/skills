# outreach — Email sequences with deliverability, caps, and breakup.

## Intake

- A-tier lead list from score mode — default: use the list as given, state the assumption it is scored, proceed.
- Offer, one proof point, and the ask (reply, meeting link) — default: one low-friction ask; interest CTAs only, never hard-close.
- Sending identity and warm-up state — default: assume cold domain, cap 30 sends per day per inbox, proceed.
- Default stack: listmonk/Mailgun sending with Kutt links for tracking where available, else any sender plus full URLs.

## Deliverable

A sequenced outbound campaign: touches with spacing, subject angles, interest CTAs, caps, reply handling, and a breakup step.

## Procedure

1. Fix sequencing first: 4–5 touches over 3 weeks (day 0, 3, 8, 15, 21); words route to `content`.
2. Touch 1 states the trigger, the relevance, and one interest CTA; follow-ups add new value, never just bump.
3. Apply caps: 30 sends per day per inbox on cold domains, ramp only after replies land without spam flags.
4. Deliverability: SPF/DKIM/DMARC verified, plain-text first touch, one link max, no attachments.
5. End every sequence in a breakup that invites a no and parks the lead with a revisit date.

## Investor-outreach variant (alternative cadence)

- Warm intros carry a forwardable blurb (<100 words): why this investor, what the company does, why now, one proof point, one ask — so the connector forwards, not rewrites.
- Short investor cadence as alternative to the 4–5-touch sequence: day 0 initial → day 4–5 follow-up with one new data point → day 10–12 final close; stop after that unless a longer sequence is requested (source: ECC `investor-outreach`).

## Quality gate

- [ ] Touch count, spacing, and caps stated.
- [ ] Every touch has an interest CTA; no hard-close.
- [ ] Deliverability checklist passed before send.
- [ ] Breakup step present with revisit date.
- [ ] Touch 1 earns its send: every sentence references specific research (real page, concrete issue, actual tech), no generic template language, never insult their current site (keeper: 99rebels/web-design-lead-qualifier).

## Routing

- Positioning or launch strategy questions → `growth`.
- Wording and copy craft → `content`.
- Status tracking and milestones → `ops`; results measurement → `analytics`.
