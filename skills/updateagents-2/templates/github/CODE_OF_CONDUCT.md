# Code of Conduct — {{PROJECT_NAME}}

## Our commitment

Project contributors are expected to make participation respectful, inclusive, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful in discussion and review.
- Critique ideas and changes without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept good-faith correction and follow project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized abuse, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Ask the project owner for a private contact if one is not already configured. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and take proportionate action, including removal from project spaces.
