## 1. Voice Profile

- **Brand Voice**: {{BRAND_VOICE}}
- **Primary Reader**: {{TARGET_AUDIENCE}}
- **Industry Context**: {{INDUSTRY}}

## 2. Operating Rules

- Lead with the answer; rationale follows.
- Use concrete verbs and verifiable claims.
- Match the client's own vocabulary from the onboarding answers.
- Stay consistent across UI copy, docs, email, and social.

## 3. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
| :--- | :--- |
| Name the exact problem: {{PROBLEM_SOLVED}} | Abstract "pain points" or "holistic challenges" |
| Show a verified workflow step | "Seamlessly integrate" without proof |
| Use the client's approved terms | Invent a new product vocabulary |

## 4. Anti-Puffery Blacklist

Never ship: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "cutting-edge", "next-generation", "unlock the potential", "in today's fast-paced landscape".

## 5. Quotability Rule

Every headline and value proposition must make complete sense within 18 words, without surrounding context.
