# 📈 growth:affiliates-referrals — Partner Programs & Viral Referral Architectures

Playbook for designing high-conversion affiliate programs, two-sided referral loops, and partner enablement assets.

---

## 1. Affiliate Program Architecture

### A. Commission Rate Calibration
- **High-Margin SaaS**: 20%–30% recurring for 12 months, or 15% lifetime.
- **Physical E-Commerce**: 10%–15% on first purchase; tiered up to 20% for top ambassadors.
- **Agency / High-Ticket Services**: 10% on first contract value, or flat referral bonus ($500–$2,000 per closed deal).

### B. Payout Cadence & Trust Rails
- **Net 30 or Net 45**: Payouts released on the 1st or 15th of each month, holding funds for the duration of the refund/chargeback window.
- **Attribution Transparency**: 60–90 day cookie window with first-touch vs. last-touch attribution explicitly documented.

### C. Partner Enablement Assets
Every successful affiliate program must supply:
1. Ready-to-use social banners, product mockups, and logo lockups.
2. Swipe copy for newsletters, X/Twitter threads, and LinkedIn posts.
3. Co-branded landing page templates with partner headshots and discount codes.

---

## 2. Referral Loop Reward Models

| Reward Model | Best For | Mechanics |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Give-Get (Two-Sided)** | Broad-appeal B2C / SaaS (Dropbox, Airbnb, Uber) | Both referrer and friend get rewarded (e.g. *"Give $20, Get $20"*). Highest viral coefficient ($K > 1$). |
| **One-Sided Reward** | Niche B2B, Developer Tools | Only the referrer receives an incentive (e.g. cash, bill credits), while the invitee gets standard onboarding. |
| **Tiered Milestone Rewards**| Newsletters, Community Platforms (Morning Brew) | Rewards unlock as referral count grows: 3 referrals (Stickers) ➔ 10 referrals (T-Shirt) ➔ 25 referrals (Exclusive AMA) ➔ 100 referrals (Private Dinner). |
| **Time-Bound Sprint Rewards**| Product Launches, Campaign Peaks | Extra cash pool or exclusive hardware awarded to top 5 referrers during a 14-day contest window. |

---

## 3. Fraud Mitigation & Anti-Abuse Controls
1. **Self-Referral Blocking**: Match device fingerprint, IP address subnet, and payment credit card hash.
2. **Minimum Payout Threshold**: Set a minimum threshold ($50–$100) before manual payout processing.
3. **PPC Brand-Bidding Ban**: Strictly forbid affiliates from bidding on branded search terms (e.g. `"[Brand Name] coupon"`) in Google Ads.
