# pr — Public Relations, Media Outreach & Crisis Communications

One unified playbook for earned media, press announcements, media pitches, executive thought leadership, and crisis communications. "The best PR isn't spin — it's truth, told well. Earned media is more credible than paid media, and speed is a competitive advantage."

---

## Intake

Before drafting any PR deliverable, confirm:
1. **The Core News Value**: What is genuinely new, counter-intuitive, or impactful to the industry? (If it only matters to internal founders, it is a blog post, not a press release).
2. **Timing & Embargo**: Is this an immediate release, or under embargo until a specific date/time (with timezone)?
3. **Target Media Tier**: Tier 1 mainstream tech (TechCrunch, Bloomberg, Verge), vertical trade press, local business journals, or podcast/newsletter curators?
4. **Authorized Spokesperson**: CEO, CTO, or designated subject-matter expert with approved bio and headshot.
5. **Active Invariants / Disclosures**: Verified metrics (funding raised, user growth, revenue) with legal sign-off. Never use unverified superlatives ("world's first", "revolutionary").

---

## Deliverable

- **Press Release**: AP-style, news-first announcement ready for newswire distribution or direct pitching.
- **Media Pitch Packet**: Tailored 3-paragraph outreach emails for journalists and editors.
- **Crisis Communications Plan**: 30-minute holding statements, stakeholder impact matrix, and reactive FAQ.
- **Executive Thought Leadership Pitch**: Bylined article outline or speaking abstract.

## Procedure

### 1. Newswire Press Release Architecture (AP Format)

```markdown
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE                          [or: EMBARGOED UNTIL: Date/Time ET]

[HEADLINE — Active voice, news angle, under 10 words, no jargon]
[SUBHEADLINE — One sentence adding quantified data or strategic context]

[CITY, STATE/COUNTRY] — [Date] — [Lead paragraph: The core news in the first 50 words. Answers Who, What, When, Where, Why, and quantified impact.]

[Context & Industry Significance: Why this matters right now. Trends driving the shift and the problem being solved.]

[Executive Quote (CEO or Founder): Strategic perspective and vision. Human, authentic voice — never corporate platitudes or throat-clearing.]

[Product / Technical Details: Concrete features, pricing, availability, ecosystem compatibility, or partnership terms.]

[Secondary Quote: Customer, partner, or accredited analyst perspective validating the claims.]

[Forward-Looking Outlook / Call to Action / Availability details.]

About [Company Name]:
[2–3 sentence boilerplate: founding date, headquarters, core mission, notable traction, website URL.]

Media Contact:
[Name] | [Title]
[Email] | [Press Kit URL]
```

#### Headline & Lead Principles
- ✅ Lead with news value, not company name: *"Acme Automates Multi-Cloud Compliance with New Open-Source Engine"* beats *"Acme Announces New Product Launch"*.
- ✅ Specific numbers beat vague assertions: *"Raises $15M Series A"* beats *"Secures Significant Growth Capital"*.
- ❌ Zero fluff adjectives: Strip out "game-changing", "disruptive", "cutting-edge", "next-generation".

### 2. High-Conversion Media Pitching

Journalists receive hundreds of pitches daily. Follow the **3-Paragraph Rule**:

```markdown
Subject: Pitch: [Target publication name] + [Specific news hook under 7 words]

Hi [First Name],

[Hook & Beat Relevance: 1–2 sentences proving you read their work.]
"Loved your recent piece on [topic]; noticed you've been tracking the shift toward [industry trend]."

[The News & Exclusive Angle: 2 sentences with concrete numbers.]
"On [Date], [Company] is launching [Product/Announcement], which [solves specific problem]. We're seeing [metric, e.g., 40% cost reduction across 12,000 beta users]."

[The Offer & Low-Friction Call-to-Action: 1 sentence.]
"I'd love to share the embargoed release and connect you with our founder [Name] for a brief 10-minute briefing under embargo until [Date/Time]. Let me know if you'd like the materials."

Best regards,
[Name]
[Contact Information + Press Kit Link]
```

- **Exclusive vs. Wide Outreach**: Offer an exclusive to your #1 priority Tier-1 reporter 5–7 days before launch. If they pass or fail to respond within 48 hours, release the embargo to targeted trade reporters.
- **Always honor embargoes**: Breaking trust with a reporter permanently burns the agency's reputation.

### 3. Crisis Communications & Holding Statements

When security breaches, outages, executive departures, or public controversies occur, execute the **30-Minute Protocol**:

#### Severity Levels (P1–P4)
| Severity | Description | SLA | Primary Channel |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **P1 — Critical** | Data breach, systemic outage, regulatory inquiry, legal action | 30 min | Public Status Page, Wire, Direct Client Email |
| **P2 — Major** | Degraded performance affecting >20% users, isolated security finding | 60 min | Status Page, In-App Banner, Support Desk |
| **P3 — Moderate** | Single feature failure, customer dispute with social escalation | 2 hours | Support Tickets, Direct DM / Outreach |
| **P4 — Low** | Minor rumor, non-impactful bug mentioned publicly | 4 hours | Monitored, internal briefing |

#### 30-Minute Holding Statement Template
```markdown
"We are actively investigating an issue regarding [briefly describe scope, e.g., service availability in the US-East region]. 

Our technical response team was mobilized immediately at [Time UTC] and is actively working toward full remediation. The security and trust of our customers remains our highest priority.

We will provide our next verified status update by [Exact Time, within 60 minutes] at [Status Page URL]."
```

#### Crisis Rules
1. **Never say "No comment"**: Signals guilt or negligence. Fill the vacuum with true, verified facts.
2. **Internal before external**: Notify employees and stakeholders before broadcasting publicly.
3. **Single spokesperson**: Route all inquiries to one designated point of contact to prevent conflicting statements.

---

### 4. Executive Thought Leadership & Bylines

Transform founders and technical leaders into recognized industry voices:
- **Angle Selection**: Avoid company promotion; focus on counter-intuitive architectural decisions, lessons learned from catastrophic failures, or future-state industry predictions.
- **Structure**:
  1. The Provocation (The consensus view is wrong).
  2. The Evidence (3 concrete real-world data points or case studies).
  3. The Framework (A named, repeatable mental model or architecture).
  4. The Practical Takeaway (What the reader should do tomorrow morning).
- **Pitching Bylines**: Pitch completed drafts or 3-bullet outlines to publications that accept contributed content (Fast Company, VentureBeat, Hacker Noon, Substack syndicates).

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Headline contains active verb and news angle without corporate jargon.
- [ ] First 50 words answer Who, What, When, Where, and Why.
- [ ] Quotes add authentic perspective rather than restating the lead paragraph.
- [ ] Embargo date, time, and timezone are explicitly declared (or clearly marked FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE).
- [ ] All claims, metrics, and partner quotes are verified and approved in writing.
- [ ] Boilerplate and media contact information are current and complete.
