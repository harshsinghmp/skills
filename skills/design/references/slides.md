# 📊 design:slides — Presentation Decks, Slide Layouts & Narrative Pitch Structures

Playbook for high-impact pitch decks, client proposals, product demos, case studies, and QBRs with proven emotional arcs and modern visual slide layout patterns.

---

## 1. When to Use
- Building investor pitch decks (Seed, Series A), sales presentations, keynote slide decks, product demos, quarterly business reviews (QBRs), and conference presentations.
- Enriching `design:story` with executive presentation design patterns.

| **YC Seed Pitch** | 10–12 slides | Early capital raise | Curiosity ➔ Frustration ➔ Hope ➔ Confidence ➔ Trust ➔ Urgency |

| **Conference Keynote** | 15–25 slides | Thought leadership & vision | Inspiration ➔ Challenge Old Paradigm ➔ New Framework ➔ Call to Greatness |

## 3. The 10-Slide YC Seed Pitch Blueprint

1. **Title / Hook**: One-sentence company categorization + punchy mission.
2. **The Problem**: Concrete, visceral pain experienced by a specific persona today (cost of status quo).
3. **The Solution**: Your product's unfair advantage or 10x breakthrough (visual hero shot).
4. **Market Opportunity**: TAM, SAM, SOM with bottom-up validation (not generic $100B hand-waving).
5. **Product & Demo**: 3 core workflow steps with screenshot annotations.
6. **Traction & Velocity**: MoM revenue/user growth curve, retention cohorts, or waitlist velocity.
7. **Business Model**: Unit economics, pricing tiers, CAC/LTV, distribution flywheel.
8. **Competitive Moat**: 2x2 matrix or comparison table focusing on your proprietary wedge.
9. **Team**: Founders' unfair domain insights, previous exits, or technical pedigree.
10. **The Ask / Use of Funds**: Exact round size, runway duration (18–24 mo), and concrete milestones to Series A.

## 4. Visual Layout Patterns

### A. The 3-Column Benefit Grid
```
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
|      Metric      |      Metric      |      Metric      |
|       +184%      |       3.2x       |       < 50ms     |
|   Organic Reach  |   Conversion Lift|   Response Time  |
|                  |                  |                  |
| Short paragraph  | Short paragraph  | Short paragraph  |
| explaining why   | explaining why   | explaining why   |
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
```

### B. The Contrast / Before-After Split
- **Left 50% (The Old Painful Way)**: Muted gray/red tones, fragmented icons, complex chaos, manual toil.
- **Right 50% (The New Automated Way)**: High-contrast brand accent, unified clean workflow, automated intelligence.

### C. The Hero Stat Callout
- Massive 72pt+ number in primary accent color.
- Single crisp sentence anchor underneath.
- Secondary supporting customer quote or data source tag.

---

## 5. Agency Slide Delivery Guardrails
1. **One Idea Per Slide**: If a slide needs more than one major takeaway, split it into two slides.
2. **Scannable in 3 Seconds**: The title should state the conclusion, not the topic (e.g. *"Revenue grew 4.2x QoQ across EMEA"* instead of *"Regional Revenue Overview"*).
3. **Typography Contrast**: Minimum 24pt for body text; minimum 44pt for headings. Never use unreadable faint gray text on projectors.
