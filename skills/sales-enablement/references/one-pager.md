# one-pager — One page that sells, scannable in 60 seconds.

## Scope

- The buyer and the single outcome the page must land.
- Proof points and a call to action.
- Who actually reads it (rep leaves it behind, buyer skims).

## Deliverable

A one-pager: the problem, the outcome, three proof points, and one CTA — laid out so a busy buyer gets the value in one scan.

## Procedure

1. Open with the buyer's problem and the outcome in one line each.
2. Show the offer: what it is and the value, not the feature list.
3. Add three proof points maximum, each verifiable.
4. Include a single CTA and next step.
5. Design for scanning: hierarchy, whitespace, one message per block.
6. Kill anything that does not serve the one outcome.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Lands the one outcome in a 60-second scan.
- [ ] Three proof points max, all verifiable.
- [ ] Single CTA and next step.
- [ ] No unsupported claims or invented social proof.

## Routing

- Claims to verify → `growth` / `analytics`.
- Visual design → `design`.
