# 🖼️ content:thumbnails — High-CTR YouTube Thumbnail Generation & Prompt Engineering

Engineering guidelines and prompt frameworks for crafting high-click-through-rate (CTR) video thumbnails using Midjourney, Imagen, and FLUX.

---

## 1. High-CTR Thumbnail Principles

1. **The 3-Element Rule**: A high-CTR thumbnail contains at most three visual elements:
   - Element 1: **The Subject** (Expressive human face, character, or hero object).
   - Element 2: **The Action / Contrast** (The focal point of curiosity, danger, or mystery).
   - Element 3: **The Minimal Text Anchor** (At most 2–4 punchy words that *complement*, not repeat, the title).
2. **Extreme Contrast**: The thumbnail must be legible when scaled down to a tiny 120px mobile preview.
3. **Complementary Title-Thumbnail Dynamic**: Never repeat the exact video title on the thumbnail. The thumbnail creates the curiosity gap; the title promises the answer.

---

## 2. Generative AI Prompt Architecture for Thumbnails

Use this exact formula when generating thumbnail assets in Midjourney or Imagen:

```text
[Subject & Facial Expression], [Action / Scenario], [Lighting & Color Grading], [Background Context], [Composition & Aspect Ratio]
```

### High-Converting Prompt Examples:

#### A. Tech / Code Breakdown:
> *"Hyper-realistic close-up portrait of a developer staring in intense realization at a glowing holographic terminal, dynamic cyan and neon magenta rim lighting, cinematic depth of field, dark modern tech studio background, 8k resolution, photorealistic, sharp focus on facial expression --ar 16:9 --style raw"*

#### B. Dramatic Business / Market Teardown:
> *"Dramatic split-composition portrait: on the left, a sleek corporate skyscraper crumbling into digital voxels; on the right, an explosive rising green holographic chart, cinematic volumetric lighting, dark moody atmosphere, ultra-detailed textures --ar 16:9 --v 6.0"*
