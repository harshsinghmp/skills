# case-study — Case study: a before/after customer story with real numbers and a quote.

## Intake

- Named customer and permission to publish
- The before-state and the specific problem
- The after-state with real metrics
- Direct quotes and the implementation path
- Default stack: Canva for layout — or any layout tool.

## Deliverable

A case study: headline with the outcome, customer snapshot, problem, approach, results with metrics, a customer quote, and a CTA — formatted for web and a PDF/summary version.

## Procedure

1. Assemble the evidence kit first: interview question bank, emotional arc (struggle → turning point → outcome), multi-format distribution spec, and a permission checklist.
2. Lead with the outcome (the headline number), not the company history.
3. Open with the before-state so the reader recognizes themselves.
4. Describe the problem and stakes concretely.
5. Walk the approach in the customer's terms, not feature jargon.
6. Show results with real, attributable metrics — no rounding that misleads.
7. Let the customer speak: use their actual quotes verbatim.
8. Close with the applicability ('if you're like them') and a CTA.
9. Get written approval on names, numbers, and quotes before publishing.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Outcome leads; numbers are real and attributable.
- [ ] Evidence kit assembled (interview bank, arc, distribution spec, permission checklist).
- [ ] A direct, verbatim customer quote is included.
- [ ] Metrics not misleading (baseline and timeframe stated).
- [ ] Names/numbers/quotes approved in writing.
- [ ] Web + summary formats both delivered.
