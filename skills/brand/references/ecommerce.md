# 🛒 brand:ecommerce — Cross-Border E-Commerce & Global Marketplace Operations Reference

> **Executive Scope**: End-to-end multi-platform cross-border e-commerce operations, marketplace onboarding (Amazon, Shopee, Lazada, Temu, TikTok Shop, AliExpress, DTC Shopify), international logistics and overseas warehousing (FBA/3PL), VAT/EPR/customs compliance, multilingual listing optimization, FX/payment settlement, and unit economics cost modeling.

---

## 1. Operating Thesis & Strategic Architecture

Cross-border e-commerce is not "taking a domestic bestseller and auto-translating it for overseas listing." 
- **Localization determines traction**: Native-speaker copywriting, local aesthetic alignment, cultural relevance, and search keyword habits.
- **Compliance determines survival**: Zero tolerance for missing certifications (CE, FCC, FDA), VAT/EPR tax evasion, or IP hijacking.
- **Supply chain determines profitability**: Accurate land-cost modeling, FBA/3PL inventory turnover, IPI governance, and FX fluctuation hedging.

## 2. Multi-Platform Marketplace Ecosystem

### A. Amazon (North America, Europe, Japan)
- **Storefront & Brand Registry**: Enroll in Amazon Brand Registry via trademark (USPTO/EUIPO/JPO) for A+ Content, Brand Story modules, Vine review enrollment, and Brand Analytics access.
- **Listing Structure Formula**:
  `[Brand] + [Core Keyword] + [Key Attribute / Material] + [Primary Selling Point / Benefit] + [Specifications / Pack Size]`
  - Bullet Points: 5 structured bullets leading with capitalized benefit keywords (`HEAVY-DUTY ALUMINUM BUILD: ...`).
  - Backend Search Terms: 249 bytes maximum, space-separated, no punctuation, no subjective claims ("best", "cheapest"), no competitor brand names.
- **A+ Content Design**: Modular storytelling with comparison charts, lifestyle infographics, and cross-sell carousels. A/B test with Manage Your Experiments (MYE).
- **FBA Inventory Discipline**: Monitor Inventory Performance Index (IPI > 450). Manage seasonal restock limits, avoid long-term storage fees (aged inventory > 180 days).

### B. Shopee & Lazada (Southeast Asia & Latin America)
- **Mega-Campaign Cadence**: 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12 Mega Sales, Payday Sales, Free Shipping campaigns.
- **Store Architecture**: Mobile-first store decoration, flash sale vouchers, bundle deals, and live streaming integration.
- **Shopee/Lazada In-App Ads**: Discovery Ads, Search Ads, and Shop Ads with weekly bid optimization against GMV and actual net profit (never chase unprofitable GMV).

### C. TikTok Shop (US, UK, SEA)
- **Creator Commerce**: TikTok Creator Marketplace affiliate outreach, commission tiering (15%-25%), sample seeding pipeline.
- **Content Operations**: Short-form hook-driven video commerce (hook in 0-2s, problem presentation, product demonstration, flash offer CTA) + scheduled LIVE shopping streams.
- **Shop Ads & Fulfillment**: Video Shopping Ads (VSA), LIVE Shopping Ads (LSA), Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) SLA compliance.

### D. Temu & Emerging Global Channels
- **Temu Operating Models**: Full-Managed (factory-direct bulk pricing, platform handles logistics/marketing) vs. Semi-Managed (local warehouse inventory, merchant handles fulfillment within 24-48h).
- **Price Competitiveness**: Strict margin modeling against factory cost; best suited for agile manufacturer-direct supply chains.

### E. Global DTC Independent Stores (Shopify / Shoplazza)
- **Multi-Currency & Localized Checkout**: Shopify Markets / Geolocation, multi-currency display, local payment gateways (iDEAL for Netherlands, Klarna/Afterpay BNPL, Boleto for Brazil, Cash on Delivery in select markets).
- **Global Fulfillment App Integration**: 3PL ERP connectors, ShipBob, YunExpress, DHL eCommerce.

## 3. International Logistics & Overseas Warehousing

### End-to-End Logistics Pipeline:
```
[ FACTORY / ORIGIN ] ──> [ FIRST-MILE FREIGHT ] ──> [ CUSTOMS CLEARANCE ] ──> [ DESTINATION FULFILLMENT ]
  Procurement & QC       - Ocean (FCL / LCL)           - HS Code classification    - FBA Inbound
                         - Air Express / Dedicated      - Import duties & tariffs  - 3PL Overseas Warehouse
                         - China-Europe Railway        - Customs bond & docs       - Local Last-Mile (USPS, DPD, Yamato)
```

### Logistics Governance & Cost Modeling:
- **Landed Cost Per Unit Formula**:
  $$\text{Landed Cost} = \text{Procurement Cost} + \frac{\text{First-Mile Freight}}{\text{Units}} + \text{Tariff/Duty} + \text{FBA/3PL Receiving Fee} + \text{Last-Mile Delivery} + \text{Storage Allocation} + \text{Return Allowance (3-5\%)}$$
- **FBA vs. 3PL Balance**: Dual-inventory strategy. Keep high-velocity 30-day stock in Amazon FBA; hold 60-day buffer in cost-effective 3PL overseas warehouse for multi-channel fulfillment (Shopify, TikTok Shop, eBay) and fast FBA restock.

## 4. Global Tax, Compliance & Regulatory Architecture

| Region / Jurisdiction | Mandatory Tax & Compliance Requirements | Risk Mitigation Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **European Union (EU)** | VAT registration, IOSS (orders < €150), OSS, German VerpackG (packaging EPR), French Triman logo, WEEE (electronics) | Appoint authorized EU representative; file monthly/quarterly VAT via certified tax agent; enroll in EPR registry. |
| **United Kingdom (UK)** | UK VAT registration, UKCA marking (where applicable), plastic packaging tax | Register for UK VAT before inventory lands in UK territory; automated HMRC filings. |
| **United States** | State Sales Tax Nexus (Economic Nexus thresholds, e.g. $100k or 200 transactions), marketplace facilitator laws | Register state-by-state when physical/economic nexus is triggered; leverage TaxJar / Avalara. |
| **Product Safety** | CE (EU), FCC (US), FDA (cosmetics, health, food-contact), PSE (Japan), CPC (Children's Product Certificate) | Test products via accredited third-party laboratories (SGS, TÜV, Intertek) before production run. |
| **Intellectual Property** | International trademark registration via Madrid Protocol or direct national filing (USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO, JPO) | Conduct clearance search on brand name & design patents before entering market; register trademark early to defeat hijackers. |

## 5. Multilingual Listing Optimization & Localization Standards

- **Native-Speaker Review Mandatory**: Auto-translated listings crater conversion rates and sound robotic or untrustworthy. Every listing must be edited or written by native speakers of the target market.
- **Target Market Keyword Harvesting**:
  - Use Helium 10 (Cerebro, Magnet) / Jungle Scout / SellerSprite to harvest local search volume, competitor keyword rankings, and high-intent long-tail phrases.
  - Account for regional vocabulary differences (e.g., US "sweater" vs. UK "jumper"; US "flashlight" vs. UK "torch"; Latin American Spanish vs. Castilian Spanish).
- **Visual Localization**:
  - Replace domestic lifestyle photos with local context and culturally representative models.
  - Format dimensions and weights in target market standards (Inches/lbs for US; Centimeters/kg for EU and Japan).
  - Infographics: clean typography, translated callouts, clear visual benefits understandable without reading text.

## 6. FX & Cross-Border Payment Infrastructure

- **Payment Collection & Virtual Accounts**: Configure regulated cross-border payment processors (PingPong, Payoneer, WorldFirst, LianLian Global) linked to multi-currency marketplace accounts (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, SGD).
- **FX Hedging & Margin Protection**:
  - Model a 3-5% exchange rate buffer into product retail pricing.
  - Schedule withdrawals during favorable currency movements; utilize forward contracts or FX limit orders for large-volume transfers.
- **Cross-Border Fee Optimization**: Negotiate withdrawal fee tiers (sub-0.4% for volume > $500k/year) to protect bottom-line margin.

## 7. Cross-Border Brand Launch & Onboarding Checklist

When a client or internal brand launches global e-commerce:

- [ ] **Corporate & IP Readiness**: International trademark registered or filed; clean brand search.
- [ ] **Platform Account Health**: Brand Registry approval on Amazon, verified TikTok Shop seller center, Shopee/Lazada Mall setup.
- [ ] **Compliance Certifications**: Lab test reports on file (CE, FCC, CPC, FDA, etc.); packaging EPR registration active.
- [ ] **Tax & Bank Configuration**: VAT numbers registered where stock is held; US sales tax automation configured; virtual multi-currency collection bank accounts connected.
- [ ] **Logistics Pipeline Verified**: Freight forwarder selected; FBA shipping plan created; 3PL warehouse integration tested; return address established in target country.
- [ ] **Localized Content Assets**: Native-speaker titles, 5 benefit-driven bullets, A+ content modules, localized lifestyle imagery, and packaging instructions translated.
- [ ] **Unit Economics Validated**: Net profit margin modeled after all landed logistics, platform commissions, ad spend target (ACOS < 25%), and return provisions.
