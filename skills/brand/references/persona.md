# 👤 brand:persona — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Persona Architecture

Actionable framework for constructing psychographic and behavioral customer personas that drive high-conversion messaging, copy, and product positioning.

---

## 1. The 7-Element Persona Framework

Every client ICP must answer these seven core elements:

1. **Role & Identity**: Job title, industry, company size, seniority level, and daily operational reality.
2. **Current Context**: What does a typical Tuesday look like? What tools do they live in? What metrics are they measured on?
3. **Visceral Pains (The Cost of Inaction)**: What causes friction, frustration, lost hours, or executive anxiety right now?
4. **Desired Outcomes**: What does "winning" look like? What outcome would earn them a promotion or take hours off their week?
5. **Buying Objections**: Why wouldn't they buy? (e.g., "Too expensive", "Migration is painful", "Security risk", "I don't trust AI").
6. **Trust Signals**: What proofs do they need to see before believing? (e.g., SOC2 badge, case studies from peer companies, live sandbox demo).
7. **Discovery & Watering Holes**: Where do they consume industry knowledge? (Subreddits, podcasts, LinkedIn newsletters, YouTube channels).

## 2. B2B SaaS Persona Blueprint Example

```markdown
### Persona: The Scaling VP of Engineering ("Scale-Up Sam")
- **Company Context**: Series B/C B2B SaaS, 50–150 engineers, migrating to microservices.
- **Top Metrics**: Sprint velocity, production uptime (99.95%), developer retention, cloud spend.
- **Core Pain**: "My senior engineers spend 30% of their sprints babysitting CI/CD and fixing flaky tests instead of shipping customer features."
- **Desired State**: Zero-maintenance deployment pipelines with automated regression rollbacks.
- **Primary Objections**: "Does this require ripping out our existing GitHub Actions workflows?" "Is our source code stored on your servers?"
- **Winning Trust Signals**: ISO/SOC2 compliance, self-hosted deployment option, benchmark tests against native tools.
- **Target Channels**: Hacker News, r/devops, Changelog podcast, Martin Fowler blog.
```
