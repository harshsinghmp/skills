# proposal — Client Proposal & Pitch Generator: B2B conversion architecture, stop-the-clock terms, and milestone proof gates.

Client-facing proposal generation and high-converting B2B pitch decks. Transform discovery briefs and technical requirements into compelling, conversion-focused commercial proposals that establish authority, protect delivery margins, and enforce clear boundaries before work starts.

---

## Intake

- **Client Profile**: Company name, industry vertical, key stakeholders, and decision makers.
- **Problem Diagnosis**: Core business pain point, revenue leak, technical bottleneck, or growth plateau.
- **Target Outcome**: Tangible business metric (e.g., +35% checkout conversion, sub-second load times, automated lead capture).
- **Proposed Scope**: Required deliverables, integrations, design assets, and architecture components.
- **Copywriting Framework**: Selected pitch structure (`PAS`, `AIDA`, or `QUEST`) based on client buying persona.
- **Commercial Bounds**: Target investment budget, milestone duration, and payment tranches.

---

## Deliverable

1. **High-Converting B2B Proposal & Pitch Deck**:
   - Executive Summary formatted for executive skim-ability and AEO/EEAT density.
   - Problem Diagnosis and Agitation using Jasper's persuasive copywriting frameworks.
   - Solution Architecture and Deliverables mapped directly to business outcomes.
2. **Definitive Scope Boundary Matrix**:
   - Explicit In-Scope inclusions vs Out-of-Scope boundaries.
   - Standard Change Request (CR) trigger thresholds.
3. **48-Hour Stop-the-Clock & Client Dependency Protocol**:
   - Contractual clause freezing project timelines when client approvals or assets are delayed.
4. **Verifiable Milestone Delivery Gates (Definition of Done)**:
   - Objective, testable acceptance criteria with cryptographic/log-level verification receipts for each milestone.
5. **Commercial Investment & Payment Schedule**:
   - Phased payment tranches linked strictly to milestone verification gates.

## 🏛️ Framework Architectures

### 1. The High-Converting B2B Proposal Formula

Select the exact narrative framework that matches the client's decision-making psychology:

| Framework | Best Used For | Psychological Arc | Tone & Stance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PAS** *(Problem-Agitate-Solve)* | Pain-conscious clients, legacy modernization, technical rescue | 1. Name the active bottleneck<br>2. Quantify cost of inaction<br>3. Present modern solution architecture | Direct, empathetic, authoritative |
| **AIDA** *(Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)* | Growth-focused clients, redesigns, new product launches | 1. Bold market hook<br>2. Competitive opportunity<br>3. Vivid transformed future state<br>4. Frictionless onboarding call-to-action | Inspiring, visionary, momentum-driven |
| **QUEST** *(Qualify-Understand-Educate-Stimulate-Transition)* | Enterprise buyers, complex technical ecosystems, consultative sales | 1. Confirm client fit<br>2. Validate systemic nuances<br>3. Demystify best practices<br>4. Reveal high-ROI leverage<br>5. Clear commercial roadmap | Consultative, analytical, rigorous |

### 3. 48-Hour Stop-the-Clock & Client Dependency Protocol

Project timelines slip when clients delay feedback, credentials, or copy. The proposal must establish the **Stop-the-Clock** invariant:

> **Standard Proposal Clause: The 48-Hour Stop-the-Clock Agreement**
> *"Our project delivery dates depend on reciprocal velocity. Any required client dependency (content submission, third-party API credentials, staging environment feedback, or milestone sign-off) that remains outstanding for more than forty-eight (48) consecutive hours triggers an automatic 'Stop-the-Clock' hold on our scheduled delivery dates.*
>
> *During a Stop-the-Clock pause, target completion milestones are automatically adjusted forward day-for-day plus a three (3) business-day rescheduling buffer to realign technical sprint allocation. If a client block exceeds fourteen (14) calendar days, the project will be placed on administrative dormancy, requiring a 10% re-activation mobilization fee before engineering resumes."*

Proposals must define how milestone completion is objectively proven so clients cannot withhold payment on subjective whim:

```markdown
### 🏁 Milestone Delivery & Acceptance Receipts

| Milestone | Deliverables | Objective Verification Receipt (DoD) | Payment Tranche |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Milestone 2: Core Engineering & CMS** | - Fully responsive page layouts<br>- Dynamic Payload CMS collections<br>- Functional API & form endpoints | - Staging URL deployment on Cloudflare/Vercel<br>- Successful test run receipt (`bun test` green)<br>- Form submission test confirmation | 35% Progress |
| **Milestone 3: Launch, QA & Handover** | - Production domain cutover<br>- Lighthouse score >= 90 mobile audit<br>- Handover documentation & training video | - Production DNS resolution receipt<br>- Passing Lighthouse audit report<br>- Signed Handover Acceptance Note | 30% Final |
```

### 5. Commercial Investment & Payment Terms

- **Fixed-Price Milestone Model**: Payments split across Milestone 1 (Deposit), Milestone 2 (Progress), and Milestone 3 (Final Delivery).
- **Payment Grace Period**: Invoices are net-7. Work on subsequent milestones does not commence until the preceding milestone invoice is cleared.
- **Post-Launch Warranty**: Every proposal includes thirty (30) days of bug-fix warranty covering code defects that violate the original specification. Enhancements and new feature requests route through standard hourly change orders or monthly retainers (`ops:retainer` / `client-comms:change`).

---

### 6. CORE-EEAT & Modern AEO Quotability for Proposals

Jasper's conversion standard: Every section header must be followed immediately by a dense, answer-first value proposition that satisfies executive scrutiny and AI quote extraction:

1. **Answer-First Density**: State the commercial benefit in the first 18 tokens beneath any major header.
2. **Quantified Outcomes**: Express value in metrics, percentages, and financial outcomes rather than effort hours.
3. **No Fluff or Corporate Filler**: Eliminate phrases like *"We are uniquely positioned"* or *"Our world-class team."* Replace with concrete proof receipts, architectural guarantees, and verifiable milestones.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Proposal specifies a concrete narrative copywriting framework (`PAS`, `AIDA`, or `QUEST`).
- [ ] Explicit Scope Boundaries table itemizes both In-Scope and Out-of-Scope deliverables.
- [ ] 48-Hour Stop-the-Clock dependency terms are included verbatim.
- [ ] Every milestone pairs deliverables with an objective, testable Definition of Done receipt.
- [ ] Payment tranches sum to exactly 100% of the project fee.
- [ ] Executive summary delivers answer-first density without corporate jargon or empty claims.
