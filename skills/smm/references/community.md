# community — Community: response playbooks, DMs, and community building.

## Intake

- Platforms and community size
- Brand voice and escalation rules
- Common question/issue types
- Response SLA and who staffs it
- Default stack: Google Business Profile + review sources Trustpilot/G2/Yelp. Fallback: ask which review surfaces the client monitors if different.

## Deliverable

A community playbook: response templates per comment type, escalation paths, an SLA, and a plan to move engaged followers into owned community (list, group, Discord).

## Procedure

1. Classify comment types: praise, question, criticism, spam, high-intent (buy/help).
2. Write templates for each type in brand voice; personalize, never paste blindly.
3. Prioritize high-intent comments and questions with a tight SLA. Answer reviews within 24h; follow the tone ladder (acknowledge by name → empathy → offline path) and escalate legal triggers (threats, injury, discrimination claims) immediately.
4. Define escalation for crises, legal, or safety issues — with a named owner. Tier it L1/L2/L3 with response clocks, a stakeholder matrix, and a zero-humor voice shift once declared.
5. Respond to criticism publicly and calmly; take specifics to DM.
6. Build a ritual (weekly prompt, Q&A, spotlight) that gives followers a reason to return.
7. Move superfans toward an owned channel you control.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Templates exist for each comment type.
- [ ] SLA defined for high-intent and question comments.
- [ ] Reviews answered within 24h on the tone ladder; legal triggers escalated.
- [ ] Escalation path with a named owner exists.
- [ ] Crisis tiered L1/L2/L3 with clocks, stakeholders, and zero-humor voice.
- [ ] Criticism handled publicly, resentments taken to DM.
- [ ] A path to owned community is defined.
