# 🔎 smm:creator-vetting — Influencer & Creator Evaluation Protocol

Comprehensive scorecard, red-flag detection, and brand safety audit protocol for sponsoring social media creators, influencers, and brand ambassadors.

---

## 1. 25-Point Creator Vetting Scorecard

Evaluate prospective creators across 5 dimensions (1–5 points each):

| Dimension | Points (1–5) | Key Evaluation Indicators |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Audience Fit** | 1–5 | Age, geography, language, and buying power align with target customer profile. |
| **2. Engagement Quality** | 1–5 | Comments demonstrate genuine conversation (not generic emoji spam or bot pods); high save/share ratios. |
| **3. Content Craft** | 1–5 | High visual production values, compelling storytelling, consistent pacing, clear audio. |
| **4. Brand Safety** | 1–5 | Clean posting history; zero controversial rage-bait, deceptive sponsored ads, or toxic drama. |
| **5. Professionalism** | 1–5 | Reliable posting cadence, clear business contact in bio, prompt communication, transparent FTC disclosures. |

### Scoring Verdict:
- **22–25 points**: **Immediate Approval** (Prime partnership candidate; prioritize multi-video retainer).
- **17–21 points**: **Conditional Approval** (Test with single sponsored post and performance kickers).
- **12–16 points**: **Hold / Further Audit** (Requires deeper audience demographic proof or price renegotiation).
- **Below 12 points**: **Hard Reject** (High risk of low ROI or brand safety hazard).

---

## 2. Creator Red Flags (Immediate Dealbreakers)

1. **Follower Spike Anomaly**: Sudden hockey-stick jumps in follower count without corresponding spikes in video views or organic comments.
2. **Engagement Pod Signatures**: Repetitive, generic comments (*"Great post! 🔥"*, *"Love this!"*) posted within minutes from accounts with zero content.
3. **Geographical Mismatch**: A creator targeting a US-focused product whose follower demographics show 80% overseas traffic from third-tier markets.
4. **Ad Saturation**: More than 50% of recent posts are sponsored brand deals, resulting in audience ad-blindness and fatigue.
5. **FTC / ASA Non-Compliance**: Hiding `#ad` or `#sponsored` tags deep below the fold or in buried hashtags.
