# ugc — UGC: source, license, and reuse user content on brand channels and ads.

## Intake

- Where UGC is needed (organic reposts, ads, website, retail)
- How UGC will be sourced (hashtag, seeding, creators)
- Rights and compensation model
- Brand-safety and quality filters
- Default stack: approved UGC reposted/scheduled via Postiz (default). Fallback: deliver reuse-ready assets usable in any scheduler.

## Deliverable

A UGC pipeline: sourcing mechanics, a rights/consent process, curation criteria, and a reuse playbook mapping content to surfaces (organic, paid, site).

## Procedure

1. Define sourcing: brand hashtag, seeding program, or paid creator content.
2. Set a rights process — written permission before any brand use, with scope (organic/paid, duration).
3. Build curation criteria: on-brand, well-lit, clear product, authentic voice.
4. Establish a lightweight system (folder/tool) tagged by product and surface.
5. Map reuse: organic repost, paid creative, website social proof, email.
6. Credit and (where appropriate) compensate contributors to sustain supply.
7. Refresh the pipeline monthly; retire stale assets.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Written rights/permission obtained before reuse.
- [ ] Curation criteria documented and applied.
- [ ] Assets tagged and mapped to surfaces.
- [ ] Contributors credited/compensated per policy.
- [ ] Paid reuse respects agreed scope and duration.
