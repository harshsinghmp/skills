# review-ask — Delight-peak review ask: capture the proof.

## Scope

- The review/testimonial ask timed at the delight peak: delivery +7d confirm or QBR close.
- This skill owns the review pipe — no clean external pipe exists, so the pattern lives here, not borrowed.
- Never ask at a low point: open incident, unpaid invoice, or unresolved complaint blocks the ask.

## Deliverable

A timed review ask (short message + direct review link) sent at a verified delight peak, with the outcome logged (review left, declined, silent).

## Procedure

1. Verify the peak: confirmed delight (positive check-in reply, QBR close, shipped win) — no peak, no ask.
2. Check the blockers: open incident, unpaid invoice, unresolved complaint → hold the ask, fix first.
3. Send the ask: short, specific (name what they loved), one direct link — friction kills reviews.
4. Log the outcome: review left (link it), declined (never re-ask this cycle), silent (one nudge max, then drop).
5. Route fresh reviews into value-notes and referral-rebuy timing — proof compounds.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Delight peak verified before send, not assumed.
- [ ] Blockers checked (incident, invoice, complaint).
- [ ] One direct link, minimal friction.
- [ ] Outcome logged; silent gets one nudge max.

## Routing

- Fix needed before ask → `ops`.
- Review earned → referral-rebuy mode follows.
- Review content reuse → `content` (testimonial assets).
