# proposal — Proposal: outcome-led pitch with a clear plan, price, and proof.

## Intake

- Prospect's problem and desired outcome (in their words)
- Scope signals and budget range
- Relevant proof (case studies, results)
- Decision-makers and timeline

## Deliverable

A proposal: problem/outcome framing, proposed approach, phased plan with deliverables, price options (good/better/best or tiered), proof, timeline, and clear next steps.

## Procedure

1. Lead with the prospect's problem and outcome, in their language, not the agency's bio.
2. Show you understand the situation better than a generic vendor (a specific insight wins).
3. Propose an approach tied to the outcome; keep it jargon-free.
4. Define phased deliverables and a realistic timeline.
5. Offer clear pricing options with what each includes (and what it does not).
6. Add proof: relevant case studies, results, references.
7. End with explicit next steps and an expiry/validity window.

## Quality gate

- [ ] Problem/outcome leads, not agency history.
- [ ] Approach tied to the stated outcome.
- [ ] Deliverables and timeline defined.
- [ ] Pricing options explicit with inclusions/exclusions.
- [ ] Relevant proof included.
- [ ] Clear next steps and validity window.
