# sow — SOW: explicit scope, deliverables, assumptions, and a change-order process.

## Intake

- Agreed scope and out-of-scope items
- Deliverables, acceptance criteria, and dates
- Assumptions and client responsibilities
- Commercial terms and the change-order process

## Deliverable

A statement of work: scope in/out, deliverables with acceptance criteria, timeline/milestones, client responsibilities, assumptions, fees and payment terms, and a change-order process.

## Procedure

1. State scope explicitly: what is included and, crucially, what is not.
2. List deliverables with acceptance criteria and due dates.
3. Record assumptions and client responsibilities (access, content, feedback turnaround).
4. Define milestones and the payment schedule tied to them.
5. Set the revision/round-count expectation to cap open-ended feedback.
6. Write the change-order process for anything beyond scope.
7. Define termination, IP, and confidentiality terms; get written agreement.

## Quality gate

- [ ] In-scope and out-of-scope stated explicitly.
- [ ] Deliverables have acceptance criteria and dates.
- [ ] Assumptions and client responsibilities recorded.
- [ ] Payment terms tied to milestones.
- [ ] Revision rounds capped.
- [ ] Change-order process defined and signed.
