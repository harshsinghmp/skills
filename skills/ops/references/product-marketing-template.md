# product-marketing-template — Shared versioned context template (shippable)

Source: `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` `product-marketing` (G3 #1). Enrich-only: no new skill; this template is the shippable capture all skills read via their local copy.

Intake rule: copy this template to `.agents/product-marketing.md` at intake, then populate §§1–11 from intake answers (mark gaps Unverified). Every skill reads the local copy before asking positioning/ICP questions. Never re-ask what is written there. Substantive edit → version bump + changelog entry (§12).

## 1. Offer (one paragraph)

- What is sold, to whom, at what value metric.

## 2. Ideal customer profile

- Firmographics, titles, triggers. Single source of truth; `gtm` research/score must not redefine without updating here.

## 3. Jobs-to-be-done + four forces

- Functional/emotional/social jobs; push/pull (toward new) vs habit/anxiety (holding back). Switching dynamics decide messaging angle.

## 4. Verbatim customer language

- Exact buyer phrases (calls, reviews, win/loss). Copy/pitch must echo these, not internal jargon.

## 5. Anti-persona

- Who this is NOT for, with disqualify signals. Saves both sides' time.

## 6. Positioning + differentiators

- Category, unique claim, proof behind it. Mirrors `growth` positioning mode; conflicts resolve here first.

## 7. Value metric + packaging

- What is charged for (seat/usage/outcome), tier fences. Mirrors `growth` pricing mode.

## 8. Proof bank

- Verifiable results, case briefs (tagged by industry/pain for `sales-enablement` retrieval), references.

## 9. Objections + honest answers

- Top objections with evidence-backed answers and graceful no-fit pivots. Mirrors `sales-enablement` objection mode.

## 10. Competitors + gaps

- Named rivals, their claims vs our cross-checked advantage. Claim cross-checks live with `growth` competitor mode.

## 11. Voice + banned phrases

- Buyer-voice rules; hype words banned from pitches and pages.
